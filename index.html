Cook Time: 25 Mins Difficulty: Easy Serving: 2 Persons

Delicious Homemade Pancakes

Welcome to my ultimate pancake recipe. These pancakes are fluffy, golden-brown, and super quick to make for a cozy morning breakfast.

Ingredients Required

1 cup All-Purpose Flour
2 tablespoons Sugar
1 tablespoon Baking Powder
1 cup Milk & 1 Egg
2 tablespoons Melted Butter

How to Prepare

1. Mix all dry ingredients together in a large bowl.
2. Whisk in the milk, egg, and melted butter until smooth.
3. Heat a skillet over medium heat and pour batter into circles.
4. Cook until bubbles form, flip, and cook until golden brown!
